VEIN Trainer
Open the VEIN Trainer shortcut. Enter a local single-player world and close inventory, menus, chat and console, then click Start. Playing × means the trainer is active; click again to stop and restore continuous physics options.
Spawn items opens the separate catalog. Choose a category, search, select an item, set quantity and add. The 1,362 item names follow the game's Chinese catalog; locations use original English names outside the Chinese interface.
Switches start OFF. OFF shows Enable; ON shows Disable. Rising sound means enabled; descending sound means disabled. One-shot actions have a single send tone, errors a low tone.
Click Set hotkey beside an option, press F1–F12 or Ctrl/Alt plus a letter or number, then Save. Bindings are saved locally and work while VEIN or this trainer is focused and the trainer is Playing. Continuous options toggle; repair/heal/teleport execute once per press.
Hold a damaged tool or weapon to repair it. The trainer verifies the same item's durability before and after repair. A repair from about 92.8% to 100% was tested in game.
Infinite stamina clears exertion only. Hunger, thirst and sleep continue normally. Speed, gravity and jump settings restore their previous values when stopped. Exit normally; forced termination may prevent restoration.
Language and light/dark theme are saved automatically. Changing either preserves active options and hotkeys. It does not change the game's language.
Target-dependent actions require aiming at the target. Giving all items, corpse removal, recipes, XP, time and teleport affect the current world; use a test save and your own backups.
Backups exclude start.vns, create verified copies under backups, and never overwrite or restore source saves. Return to the main menu before backing up.
This build supports the verified local VEIN installation and single-player version only. Version mismatch disables editing. Repair, live condition readback, physics restoration and Windows hotkey registration/message handling have been checked; not every game effect or sustained stamina use has been tested in game.

All non-item actions use the included native connection module on the game thread without opening the console or simulating keystrokes. Item spawning keeps its console sender. Direct healing and held-item repair were tested; other world effects were not all triggered. No game files or saves are rewritten by the bridge.
